Why NaviTom

Less universal gadget. More motorcycle.

Most motorcycle screens stop at phone mirroring. NaviTom is being designed as a model-aware instrument platform: navigation in the centre, useful bike data around it, and software profiles that improve as more motorcycles are validated.

01 · NAVIGATION

Phone projection, shaped for a round screen

CarPlay / Android Auto connectivity with a motorcycle-first layout instead of a rectangular car UI squeezed onto the bars.

02 · TELEMETRY

Use the bike data that actually exists

Passive vehicle telemetry where validated, plus GPS and optional sensors. Unsupported values stay unsupported — they are never invented.

03 · ADAPTATION

Guided AI setup

The planned setup assistant helps identify the bike, rank compatible signal mappings and configure a suitable dashboard profile. Deterministic validation decides what is safe to accept.

Setup concept

Connect. Observe. Calibrate. Validate. Ride.

The AI is a setup assistant — not an autonomous motorcycle controller. V1 keeps motorcycle CAN communication passive/read-only.

1 · PairPhone, NaviTom hardware and supported telemetry adapter connect.
2 · IdentifySelect motorcycle make, model, year and current hardware profile.
3 · ObservePassive signals are captured while the rider follows safe calibration prompts.
4 · ValidateKnown profiles, ranges and consistency checks reject uncertain mappings.
5 · ConfigureNaviTom installs the supported dashboard and records its compatibility evidence.
